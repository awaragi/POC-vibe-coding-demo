We need a maintenance work order management system tailored for a manufacturing plant with complex equipment and spare parts dependencies. The system should allow operators to raise maintenance requests when machines show faults or performance degradation. Maintenance planners need to convert requests into work orders with assigned technicians, required parts, and estimated effort. Each work order should track status from Requested to In Progress, Waiting for Parts, and Completed. The system should maintain an inventory of spare parts and automatically flag shortages or reorder needs. Technicians should record work performed, parts used, and time spent directly in the system. Historical work orders should be linked to equipment assets for analysis. Dashboards should show backlog, response times, and maintenance costs. The goal is to streamline maintenance operations and reduce downtime caused by poor planning or missing parts.
