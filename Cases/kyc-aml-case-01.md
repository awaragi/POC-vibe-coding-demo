We need a KYC/AML compliance system for banking to verify customer identities and prevent money laundering. The system should allow customers to submit onboarding documents like ID and proof of address, then automatically verify them against watchlists and perform risk assessments. Bank officers need to review flagged high-risk cases and approve low-risk ones quickly. Post-onboarding, the system should monitor transactions for suspicious activities, such as unusual transfers or large deposits. The goal is to ensure regulatory compliance, reduce fraud, and speed up customer onboarding from days to minutes.
