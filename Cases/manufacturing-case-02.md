We need an equipment health and hardware status monitoring application for a manufacturing environment with critical machines on the shop floor. The system should collect and display real-time status indicators such as running, idle, fault, and maintenance mode for each piece of equipment. Operators should be able to view machine health at a glance and report anomalies or warnings when thresholds are exceeded. Maintenance teams need alerts when equipment shows early signs of failure, such as abnormal vibration or temperature trends. Each asset should have a lifecycle history including past failures, repairs, and downtime. The system should support prioritization of maintenance tasks based on business impact. Managers need dashboards showing overall equipment effectiveness and availability. Integration with maintenance workflows should allow quick assignment and tracking of repair tasks. The goal is to reduce unplanned downtime and move from reactive to predictive maintenance.
