We need an incident and safety report management system for a manufacturing plant to track, investigate, and resolve operational and safety incidents. The system should allow operators and supervisors to quickly log incidents such as equipment malfunctions, near misses, or workplace injuries using structured forms and optional photos. Each incident should be categorized by severity, location, equipment involved, and potential root cause. Safety managers need to review incidents, assign investigations, and track corrective actions to completion. The system should support status tracking from Reported to Under Investigation, Mitigated, and Closed. Dashboards should highlight recurring incidents and high-risk areas across the plant. Notifications should alert responsible teams when incidents are assigned or overdue. Historical data should be searchable for audits and compliance reporting. The goal is to improve plant safety, reduce downtime, and ensure regulatory compliance through better visibility and accountability.
